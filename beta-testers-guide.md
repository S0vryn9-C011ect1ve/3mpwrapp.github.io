# Beta Testers Guide

Thank you for helping shape 3mpwrApp. Beta testers are a vital part of building an app that truly works for disabled people and injured workers.

## What Beta Means

The app is in active development. As a beta tester you get early access to features before they reach the public app stores. Some features may change, and you may hit rough edges — that feedback is exactly what we need.

## Joining the Beta

- **Waitlist**: join at [app waitlist](/app-waitlist/)
- **Web app**: the latest build is always available at [app.3mpwrapp.ca](https://app.3mpwrapp.ca)
- **Store builds**: released as production readiness is confirmed

## How to Give Great Feedback

1. Note **what you were doing** when something happened
2. Note your **device** and **screen-size / accessibility settings**
3. Say whether it was a **bug**, a **confusing moment**, or a **feature request**
4. Use the in-app **Send** button on the About screen, or email through the app

Good feedback is specific: *"On the Wellness tab, the mood buttons were too small at 200% text scaling"* helps far more than *"the app is broken."*

## What We Test For

- Accessibility (screen readers, contrast, text scaling)
- Offline reliability
- Plain-language clarity
- Privacy respecting behavior

## Your Privacy as a Tester

- Beta builds respect the same **no-tracking** rule as production
- Your test data stays on your device unless you choose to share it
- You can delete your data anytime

## What's Off-Limits to Share

Please do not post other organizations' private contact emails or internal content you encounter. If you spot a third-party email in the app, report it to us and we'll remove it.
