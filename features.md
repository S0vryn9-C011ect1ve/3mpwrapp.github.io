# 3mpwrApp Features

3mpwrApp is built for disabled people, injured workers, caregivers, and allies across Canada. It is **offline-first**, **accessibility-first** (WCAG AAA target), and **privacy-respecting** — your data stays on your device unless you choose to sync.

Below are the features available in the current app build.

## Core Screens

- **Home** — Your personalized starting point with next-step suggestions and quick access to tools.
- **AI Assistant** — Ask questions about your rights, benefits, and advocacy in plain language. Built to help you navigate systems, not replace professional advice.
- **Advocacy** — Campaign coordination, accountability tracking, and communication scripts to organize for change.
- **Campaigns** — Discover and join active campaigns (including community events like the Thunder Bay & District Injured Workers Tuesday Info Sessions).
- **Community** — Connect with peers, mentors, and supporters.
- **Events** — Calendar of injured-worker sessions, workshops, and community meetings.
- **Research** — Evidence-based resources backed by CANLII data and tribunal decisions, so you can ground your case in real outcomes.
- **Resources** — Curated support tools, legal self-representation guides, and crisis resources.
- **Saved** — Bookmark articles, decisions, and resources for later.
- **Wellness** — Mood tracking and wellness check-ins that respect your privacy.
- **Profile / Settings** — Manage your account, text scaling, high-contrast mode, and privacy controls.
- **FAQs** — Answers to common questions about the app and your rights.
- **Voice Help** — Hands-free, screen-reader-friendly assistance.
- **Inbox** — Your messages and notifications.

## Accessibility First

Every screen is designed to be screen-reader navigable, with:

- Adjustable text scaling (up to 200%)
- High-contrast mode
- Reduced-motion support
- Clear, plain-language copy
- Keyboard and switch navigation paths

## Privacy & Data Ownership

- Offline-first: core tools work without a connection
- Your data belongs to you — export or delete anytime
- No third-party analytics or tracking
- Opt-in, not opt-out, for any cloud features
