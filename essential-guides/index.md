# Essential Guides

You shouldn't have to hunt through confusing government websites, or wonder whether
the page you're reading is still current.

**Everything linked below is real and written.** This page used to advertise guides
under `/essential-guides/…` that had never been created — every one was a dead link,
and the same topics were listed twice with conflicting slugs. Those have been replaced
with the guides that actually exist, organised by the situation you are in.

## Start here: which system applies to you?

Most people apply to the wrong program first and lose weeks to it. **Two minutes
here saves a month.**

**→ [Which System Applies to Me](/knowledge-base/which-system-applies-to-me/)**

It routes by situation — workplace injury, service injury, or neither — to the
programs you might actually qualify for.

## ⚠️ A note on currency — and on what this page used to claim

Programs change. Benefit amounts, thresholds and appeal deadlines shift, often
annually. Every page shows its own **Last Updated** date.

**Always confirm a figure against the official page before relying on it.** Where a
rule changes frequently, we say so on the page rather than pretending otherwise.

**This page previously stated that guides were reviewed monthly, quarterly, and
"real-time." No such review process existed.** Those claims were untrue and have been
removed rather than left standing. Reviews happen when they happen, and each page
now carries a date so you can judge for yourself.

*This is legal information, not legal advice. For anything contested or
time-sensitive, get representation.*
