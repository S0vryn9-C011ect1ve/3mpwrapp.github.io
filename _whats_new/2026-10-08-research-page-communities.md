What we shipped:

- The **/research/** page now links guides, knowledge bases, and appeal templates for **every community we serve** — injured workers (WSIB/WSIAT), veterans (VAC/VRAB), seniors, caregivers, persons with disabilities, and the human-rights / UNCRPD / legal communities.
- Added a **"Communities We Serve"** section that groups all resources by community, so people can jump straight to what applies to them.
- Added a note that **CanLII decision-data scraping is resuming**: British Columbia WCAT 2020–2026 is already collected (7,386 decisions via the free CanLII API); next is the rest of BC, then all of Canada plus the ON Landlord & Tenant Board. Collection runs on the free CanLII API (15-second rate limit) — no paid Firecrawl credits used (conserved at 95%).

Why it matters: research is now a single hub that connects the audit's evidence to the guides, KBs and templates every community can actually use — and the flywheels that power the ops dashboard are fed from the same scraped decision data.
