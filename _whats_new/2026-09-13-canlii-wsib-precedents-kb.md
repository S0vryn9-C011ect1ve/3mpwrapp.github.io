The CanLII WSIB Precedents knowledge base is live — a curated library of landmark WSIAT/WSIB decisions shaping injured-worker rights in Ontario, built from 98,992 cases. It surfaces the documented "unemployable" denial pattern (verified through @fightthewcb's thread), the Ontario Court of Appeal ruling blocking WSIB "criminalization" of recovery, and the "competitively unemployable" definition gap — all cross-linked to our [Bill 105 knowledge base](/knowledge-base/bill-105-wsib-lockin/). This is what powers 3mpwrApp's Evidence Flywheel and Athena's case-corpus monitoring.


---

**Data note (2026-10-15):** Figures citing "98,992" refer to the *WSIAT Open Data Portal's* published-decision metadata. The live Portal count was **96,025** as of 2026-10-07. 3mpwrapp's locally outcome-classified corpus is **11,430** ONWSIAT decisions (`onwsiat-outcomes-3-tier-consolidated.json`, 2026-04-26): 649 resolved (5.7%), 73.5% worker-win among decisive outcomes, 94.3% unresolved.
