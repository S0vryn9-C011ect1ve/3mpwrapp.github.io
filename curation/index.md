# Daily News Curation

A short daily round-up of disability rights, accessibility, benefits, and social policy
news from across Canada — written for disabled people, injured workers, and the people
who support them.

Each entry links out to the original source, so you can read the full story and check
the claim yourself. Nothing here is a summary you have to trust — it is a signpost.

## Why this exists

News about disability policy is mostly written for institutions. This is the same
material, gathered daily and pointed at the people it actually affects.

## About these round-ups

These are generated daily from a fixed set of Canadian disability, accessibility, and
social policy sources, then published with a short excerpt and a link to the original.
We do not rewrite the source material, and we do not add commentary we cannot support.

If a story matters to you and is missing, the [contact page](/contact/) is the way to
reach us.
