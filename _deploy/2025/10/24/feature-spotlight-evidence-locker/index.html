Photos are automatically organized by date. When your lawyer asks for incident documentation a year later, you have timestamped proof you documented it contemporaneously - much stronger evidence than reconstructed records. For Benefit Applications: CPP-D, ODSP, DTC - all require medical evidence. Instead of scrambling to gather documents from five different offices, you’ve been collecting them as you go. Application deadline hits? You’re ready in 20 minutes instead of 20 hours. For Accommodation Requests: Store every email about your workplace accommodation, every occupational therapist report, every modification you’ve tried. When you need to escalate, you have complete documentation of efforts made. For Legal Proceedings: Your lawyer needs specific medical reports for a human rights complaint. Instead of “I think it’s in my email somewhere,” you hand over a secure link with exactly what’s needed. No fishing.
