Peer Support Matching is designed to connect with others who share similar disabilities, experiences, and challenges. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp’s commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada. * * * ## Key Highlights - Match algorithm considers disability type, experiences, location
