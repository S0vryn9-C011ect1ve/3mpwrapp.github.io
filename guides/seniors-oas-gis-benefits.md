# Seniors Benefits in Canada: OAS, GIS, Allowance, CPP Disability & GAINS

*Federal pensions and credits for people 60 and older, plus Ontario's GAINS top-up — and what to do when a decision is wrong.*

## Executive Summary

If you are 60 or older and your income is low to modest, you may be entitled to **several** layers of government money at once. Most people only ever hear about OAS, so they leave GIS, the Allowance, and Ontario's GAINS on the table.

**The layering that matters:**
1. **OAS** — a pension almost everyone 65+ gets (taxable).
2. **GIS** — a tax-free top-up you can only get *if you already get OAS* and have low income.
3. **Allowance** — for spouses aged 60–64 of someone getting GIS.
4. **GAINS** (Ontario only) — a non-taxable top-up on top of OAS + GIS.
5. **CPP Disability** — for people under 65 who are severely disabled; converts to a CPP retirement pension at 65.

These are **not** mutually exclusive. You can receive OAS + GIS + GAINS together, and CPP Disability alongside them if you are under 65.

**Three things to know before you start:**
1. **You rarely have to apply for OAS/GIS if you are already in the system** — Service Canada usually enrolls you automatically the month after your 64th birthday. If no letter arrives, apply.
2. **GIS and GAINS both stop or shrink if you do not file your tax return every year.** Filing is how they confirm your income.
3. **Every one of these decisions can be appealed** — OAS/GIS/CPP go to the Social Security Tribunal of Canada; GAINS goes to the Ontario Ministry of Finance. Deadlines are short (90 days). Act fast.

## Old Age Security (OAS) — Canada-wide

- **Who:** 65 or older; Canadian citizen or legal resident who has lived in Canada **at least 10 years since age 18** (20 years if living outside Canada).
- **Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:**
  - Age **65–74: $762.50/month**
  - Age **75+: $838.75/month** — permanently 10% higher since July 2022
- **Taxable.** A recovery tax (clawback) applies if your 2025 net world income is above **$93,454**; amounts are indexed to CPI quarterly (they go up, never down).
- **Apply:** usually automatic. If no enrolment letter arrives a month after your 64th birthday, apply through [My Service Canada Account](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/my-account.html) or a Service Canada office.
- **Appeal:** request reconsideration within **90 days** (form **ISP-3134**) → **Social Security Tribunal of Canada**. See [Appealing a Federal Benefits Decision](#appealing-a-federal-benefits-decision).
- Source: [Old Age Security — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security.html)

## Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) — Canada-wide

A **tax-free** monthly top-up. You must **receive OAS** to get it.

**Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:**

| Who | Maximum / month |
|---|---|
| Single, widowed or divorced | **$1,138.90** |
| Spouse/common-law partner receives full OAS | **$685.56** |
| Spouse receives the Allowance | **$685.56** |
| Spouse receives **no** OAS or Allowance | **$1,138.90** |

GIS is clawed back as your income rises, so the amounts above are the maximum at the lowest income. **File your taxes every year** — GIS stops or drops if you do not.

- Source: [Guaranteed Income Supplement — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/guaranteed-income-supplement.html)

## The Allowance and Allowance for the Survivor

**Allowance** — for people **60 to 64** whose spouse/common-law partner receives GIS.

- **Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:** up to **$1,428.06/month** (spouse receives GIS and full OAS).
- Apply **6 to 11 months before you turn 60** (or as soon as possible if already 60–64).
- Source: [Allowance — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/guaranteed-income-supplement/allowance.html)

**Allowance for the Survivor** — for low-income widowed people aged 60–64 who are not yet eligible for OAS. You are automatically moved onto it if you were receiving the Allowance and your spouse dies.

## CPP Disability for Seniors

CPP Disability is for people **over 18 and under 65** who have a severe and prolonged disability that prevents substantially all work. (At 65 it converts to a CPP retirement pension — it does not continue as a disability benefit.)

- **Maximum amount (2026): $1,741.20/month**
- **Average amount (July 2026): $1,236.32/month**
- **Apply** with a medical form completed by your doctor or nurse practitioner, through My Service Canada Account.
- **Appeal:** if refused, request **reconsideration** with Service Canada (form **ISP-1145**, "Request for Reconsideration of a CPP Disability…"), then appeal to the **Social Security Tribunal of Canada — General Division** within **90 days** of the reconsideration decision.
- If you are **under 65, already receiving a CPP retirement pension**, and become severely disabled, you may qualify for the **CPP Post-Retirement Disability Benefit** instead.
- Sources: [CPP Disability — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/cpp-disability-benefit.html) · [Social Security Tribunal of Canada](https://sst-tss.gc.ca/en)

> Note: CPP Disability is for people **under 65**. Seniors 65+ who are disabled are supported through OAS + GIS + GAINS and (if applicable) a CPP retirement pension, not CPP Disability.

## GAINS — Ontario's Top-Up

The **Ontario Guaranteed Annual Income System** adds a **non-taxable** monthly payment on top of OAS and GIS for low-income Ontario seniors.

- **Who:** age 65+, Ontario resident (lived in ON 12 of the past 24 months, or 20 years since age 18), receiving OAS **and** GIS, with **private income** of **$4,416 or less** (single) or **$8,832 or less** (couple) in the reference year.
- **Maximum, 2026 benefit year (Jul 1, 2026 – Jun 30, 2027):** up to **$92/month** (single) or **$184/month** (couple). The exact amount is reduced as private income rises.
- **Apply:** automatic if you already get OAS + GIS and file your taxes. You must **file your income tax return every year by April 30** (or file a GIS application) to keep getting it.
- **Payments:** issued around the 25th of each month, July to June.
- **Appeal:** if you disagree with a GAINS decision, call the Ontario Ministry of Finance at **1-866-668-8297 (ONT-TAXS)**; if still unsatisfied, file a **Notice of Objection** within **90 days** of the written decision.
- Source: [GAINS payments for seniors — ontario.ca](https://www.ontario.ca/page/guaranteed-annual-income-system-payments-seniors)

## Long-Term Care, Capacity & CCB Appeals

Two different Ontario bodies handle elder-care disputes — do not mix them up:

- **Consent and Capacity Board (CCB)** — hears disputes about a person's **mental capacity, consent to treatment, psychiatric committal, and findings of incapacity to make long-term-care decisions**. This is the board the task referenced as "CCB." Its site is **not** under tribunalsontario.ca; it is at **[ccboard.on.ca](https://www.ccboard.on.ca/)**.
- **Long-term care home placement / admission appeals** — disputes about a placement-coordinator decision under the *Fixing Long-Term Care Act, 2021* are appealed to the **Health Services Appeal and Review Board (HSARB)**, whose functions are administered within **[Tribunals Ontario](https://tribunalsontario.ca/en)**. Confirm the assigned tribunal when you file.

If you are unsure which body hears your matter, the **Advocacy Centre for the Elderly (ACE)**, Ontario's specialist elder-law clinic, is a free resource: [acelaw.ca](https://www.acelaw.ca/).

> ⚠️ The slug `tribunalsontario.ca/ccb` returns a 404. The CCB's official site is **ccboard.on.ca**. We link there instead of a dead URL.

## Appealing a Federal Benefits Decision

For **OAS, GIS, CPP or CPP Disability**, the route is the same shape:

1. **Reconsideration** with Service Canada (or CPP for disability). Deadline: **90 days** from the decision. Forms: **ISP-3134** (OAS/GIS) or **ISP-1145** (CPP Disability).
2. If the reconsideration still refuses you, appeal to the **Social Security Tribunal of Canada** within **90 days** of that decision. OAS/GIS appeals go to the General Division; CPP Disability also goes to the General Division.
3. You can represent yourself, bring a trusted person, or appoint a representative.

A fill-in letter you can adapt is in our [Senior Benefits Appeal template](/templates/senior-benefits-appeal/). A shorter reference version lives in the [Seniors Benefits Canada knowledge-base article](/knowledge-base/seniors-benefits-canada/).

## Appealing a GAINS Decision

1. Call the Ontario Ministry of Finance: **1-866-668-8297**.
2. If unresolved, file a **Notice of Objection** (Ontario form) within **90 days** of the mailed decision, by registered mail to:
   > Ministry of Finance
   > c/o Director, Advisory, Objections, Appeals and Services Branch
   > 33 King Street West, P.O. Box 699, Station A
   > Oshawa, Ontario L1H 8S6
