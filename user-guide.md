# 3mpwrApp User Guide

Welcome to 3mpwrApp. This guide walks you through getting started, using each tool, and adjusting the app for your needs.

## Getting Started

1. **Download** the app from your app store (or open the [web app](https://app.3mpwrapp.ca)).
2. **Open Home** — your starting point with suggested next steps.
3. **Set your preferences** in *Settings*: text size, high-contrast mode, and reduced motion.

## Navigating the App

The bottom tab bar gives you one-tap access to the main areas:

- **Home** — overview and quick actions
- **AI Assistant** — ask anything in plain language
- **Advocacy** — campaigns and accountability tools
- **Campaigns / Community / Events** — get involved
- **Research / Resources** — evidence and support
- **Saved** — your bookmarks
- **Wellness** — mood and check-ins
- **Profile / Settings** — your controls

## Using Key Features

### AI Assistant
Tap the AI Assistant tab and type your question — for example, "What are my rights after a workplace injury in Ontario?" The assistant explains in plain language and points you to resources. It does not give legal advice.

### Research & Resources
Browse CANLII-backed tribunal decisions and self-representation guides. Tap **Save** on anything you want to revisit.

### Events & Campaigns
Find injured-worker info sessions and community campaigns. The **Thunder Bay & District Injured Workers Tuesday Info Sessions** run most Tuesdays (holidays and summer break excepted) — join via the link on each event.

### Wellness
Use the Wellness tab to log your mood. Entries stay on your device.

## Accessibility Settings

- **Text scaling**: Settings → Display → Text Size (up to 200%)
- **High contrast**: Settings → Display → High Contrast
- **Reduced motion**: enabled automatically if your device requests it
- **Voice Help**: tap the voice-help button on any supported screen

## Privacy

- Core features work **offline** — no connection needed
- Your data is **yours** — export or delete anytime from Settings
- No third-party trackers

## Getting Help

- In-app: tap **Help** on any screen
- Website: [Support](/support/)
