# WSIB Strike Alert: Appeals Paused — What Injured Workers Need to Know

*Published September 23, 2026 | Sources: Ontario Federation of Labour, Injured Workers Online, Hicks Morley LLP, Matthews Dinsdale LLP*

**If you have a WSIB appeal or WSIAT hearing scheduled — listen up.**

Since May 22, 2025, over **4,000 WSIB employees** have been on strike. The WSIB has **suspended all appeals processes** for the duration of the labour disruption. If you're waiting on a hearing, a reconsideration decision, or a claim update, you are not alone — and you are not missing anything.

---

## What's Actually Paused

- **WSIAT hearings** — postponed pending claim file updates from WSIB
- **WSIB Reconsiderations** — suspended
- **New hearing dates** — not yet scheduled; WSIB will contact you
- **Routine claim processing** — operating at minimal capacity

## What's Still Moving

- **Urgent medical reports** — still being processed
- **Immediate benefit payments** — continuing (CPP-D, disability payments, etc.)
- **Online services** — My Benefits portal, eForms, etc.

## The Bigger Picture

The strike isn't just about WSIB workers' working conditions — it's about **access to justice for injured workers**. When WSIB can't staff its offices, process claims, or update claim files, the entire system slows down.

And in September 2026, the situation grew worse: reports confirm 400+ WSIB jobs cut, with offices closing in Thunder Bay, St. Catharines, and Sudbury — and critical IT/claims functions being relocated to Texas and Boston.
