# OFL × ONIWG Injured & Ill Worker Conference — Oct 1

*Published September 23, 2026 | [Register here](https://ofl.ca/event/injured-and-ill-worker-conference-2026-registration)*

## What

The **Ontario Federation of Labour (OFL)** and the **Ontario Network of Injured Workers Groups (ONIWG)** are co-hosting an all-day **Injured & Ill Worker Conference**.

This is the collective-action conference that matters right now — with the WSIB strike ongoing and Bill 105 threatening the lock-in, the message is clear: **organized workers win**.

## When & Where

- **Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Time:** 9:30 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Venue:** IBEW Local 353 Hall, 1377 Lawrence Ave. East, Toronto (M3A 3P8)

## Why This Matters for You

- **WSIB strike updates** — over 4,000 WSIB workers have been on strike since May 2025; appeals are paused
- **Bill 105 fallout** — the lock-in removal debate continues; the Legislature returns October 27
- **Network with other injured workers** — connect with ONIWG groups across Ontario
- **Organized resistance** — union-backed advocacy that actually moves the needle

## Registration

**Free.** Register at: [https://ofl.ca/event/injured-and-ill-worker-conference-2026-registration](https://ofl.ca/event/injured-and-ill-worker-conference-2026-registration)

Registration opened with OFL's announcement. The September 16 cutoff may have passed for guaranteed seating, but **check with OFL directly** — space is often released closer to the event.
