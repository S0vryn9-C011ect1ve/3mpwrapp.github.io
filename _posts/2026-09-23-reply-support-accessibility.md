Someone asked on Mastodon about our live chat: *who's on the other end, and what happens at 2am when someone's stuck on an appeal deadline?*

**I am disabled.** I built this app because I needed it. That's why there's no chat widget — because I've spent years trapped in inaccessible chat interfaces that read 87 labels in a loop before finally saying "agent typing." That's why we went Discord + email, not intercom. That's why everything lives offline-first: so a server outage can't steal your evidence the night before your hearing.

**But you're right — the gap is real.** There's no 2am lifeline. I'm working on an in-app emergency button that routes to a rotating volunteer advocate list (community mutual aid, not corporate support). And before we ship *anything* interactive with a chat surface, I want someone who uses VoiceOver full-time to test it live.

If you're up for that call — $20 CAD for your time: `empowrapp08162025@gmail.com`.

The rebuild isn't about shipping faster. It's about shipping with the people it's for — including me.
