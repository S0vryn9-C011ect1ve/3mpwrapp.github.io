# Legislation

Key legislation affecting injured workers and disability rights.

## Ontario — Workers' Compensation

| Law | What it does |
|---|---|
| **WSIA 1997** (S.O. 1997, c. 12) | The Ontario workers' compensation statute — establishes the WSIB and the WSIAT |
| **Bill 86 — Meredith Act** (Fair Compensation for Injured Workers), 2025 | Compensation reform. See [Bill 86 analysis](/knowledge-base/bill-86-meredith-act/) |
| **Bill 105** | Proposed WSIB "lock-in" removal. See [Bill 105 explained](/knowledge-base/bill-105-wsib-lockin/) |

### SIEF — CLOSED
The **Second Injury and Enhancement Fund** **closed effective 16 June 2026** (WSIB
Operational Policy Manual), announced 16 July 2026 after a value-for-money audit. WSIB
is no longer accepting new SIEF requests and can only review requests received before
16 July 2026. **This does not affect any right to appeal a claim decision.**
[WSIB — SIEF policy](https://www.wsib.ca/en/operational-policy-manual/second-injury-and-enhancement-fund-sief)

## British Columbia — Workers' Compensation

### Bill 41 — Amendments to the Workers Compensation Act
**In force since 1 January 2024** — not proposed.
[WorkSafeBC](https://www.worksafebc.com/en/law-policy/workers-compensation-law/amendments/bill-41-amendments-to-the-workers-compensation-act/employers-duty-to-cooperate-duty-to-maintain-employment)

- **Duty to cooperate** — employers and workers must cooperate in a timely and safe
  return to work, and with WorkSafeBC to identify suitable work.
- **Duty to maintain employment** — applies **only** to employers who regularly employ
  **20 or more workers** and employed the injured worker for **at least one year**
  before the injury.
- Employers must make changes needed to accommodate the worker **unless this creates
  undue hardship**.
- **Retroactive reach:** duty to cooperate applies to injury dates up to **2 years**
  before 1 Jan 2024; duty to maintain employment up to **6 months** before.
- **Consequence of non-compliance:** WorkSafeBC may apply an administrative penalty based
  on the wage-loss or other benefits paid to the worker.

### Compensation-Related Maximum Wage Rate
Set annually by the President under s.209 of the *Workers Compensation Act*.

| Year | Maximum wage rate | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| **2027** | **$130,800.00** | 1 Jan 2027 |
| **2026** | **$127,500.00** | 1 Jan 2026 |
| 2025 | $121,500.00 | 1 Jan 2025 |
| 2024 | $116,700.00 | 1 Jan 2024 |

[WorkSafeBC — Maximum Wage Rates](https://www.worksafebc.com/en/law-policy/claims-rehabilitation/compensation-related-maximum-wage-rates)

> ⚠️ **Commonly-cited but outdated:** the "$87,100 → $100,000" increase is the **2023**
> change. Current maximum insurable wage figures are **$127,500 (2026)** and
> **$130,800 (2027)**.
