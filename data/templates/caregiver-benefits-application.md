# Caregiver Benefits Application Template

**FOR PEOPLE CARING FOR A SICK, DISABLED OR DYING FAMILY MEMBER OR FRIEND**

Use this to (1) apply for EI caregiving benefits and (2) track the tax credits you can claim at tax time.

**📋 INSTRUCTIONS:** Fill in the [BRACKETS]. Delete what does not apply. Keep every receipt and letter.

---

## PART 1 — WHICH EI CAREGIVING BENEFIT?

| Benefit | Weeks | The person you care for |
|---|---|---|
| Family caregiver benefit for children | up to 35 | Critically ill/injured child under 18 |
| Family caregiver benefit for adults | up to 15 | Critically ill/injured person 18+ |
| Compassionate care benefit | up to 26 | Person needing end-of-life care |

You **do not** need to be related or live with the person — only "like family," with a medical certificate.

**Benefit I am applying for:** [choose one]
**Person cared for:** [name], relationship: [like-family relationship]
**Medical certificate completed by:** Dr. [name], dated [date]

## Appeal note (if EI refuses)

Request **reconsideration within 30 days** (form **INS5210**), no fee. Since **April 1, 2026** EI appeals go to the **EI Benefits Appeal Tribunal (BOA)**, not the SST.
