# Human-Rights Complaint Template (HRTO / CHRT)

Use this to structure a discrimination complaint. Fill in the brackets `[ ]`. Two tracks:

- **Ontario (HRTO):** file **Form 1** directly with the HRTO. Deadline: generally **one year**
  from the last incident. Free. Get help from the [Human Rights Legal Support Centre](https://hrlsc.on.ca/).
- **Federal (CHRT):** file a **complaint with the Canadian Human Rights Commission (CHRC)**
  first; it may refer to the CHRT. Covers federal employers, banks, telecom, transport, First Nations.

> This is a template, not legal advice. Name the protected ground, the incidents, and the
> link to discrimination. Keep a copy of everything you submit.
