# Caregivers Support Canada — Quick Reference

**Verified 2026-10-07** against official sources, linked throughout. **Not legal or tax advice.**

> 🔎 Caring is unpaid work most people never claim. You can often claim federal income support, a tax credit, and a tax credit for medical costs — at the same time.

---

## EI Caregiving Benefits — Canada-wide

Three benefits; all pay **55% of earnings, max $729/week (2026)**.

| Benefit | Max weeks | For |
|---|---|---|
| Family caregiver benefit for children | 35 | Critically ill/injured child under 18 |
| Family caregiver benefit for adults | 15 | Critically ill/injured person 18+ |
| Compassionate care benefit | 26 | Person needing end-of-life care |

- You **do not** need to be related or live with the person — just "like family" + medical certificate.
- Weeks can be shared by multiple caregivers.
- **Apply:** My Service Canada Account. **Appeal:** reconsideration within **30 days** (form **INS5210**) → EI Benefits Appeal Tribunal (BOA) (since Apr 1 2026).
- [EI caregiving — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/caregiving.html)

## Canada Caregiver Credit — federal tax credit

- Non-refundable credit up to **$8,601** (2025 tax year) on **line 30425** for an infirm spouse/partner or eligible dependant 18+.
- Reduced as the dependant's income rises; indexed annually.
- [Canada caregiver credit — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/canada-caregiver-amount.html)

## Medical Expense Tax Credit (METC) — federal

- Claim eligible medical expenses on **line 33099** (you, spouse, kids <18) or **33199** (dependants).
- Deduct the **lesser of 3% of net income or $2,834** (2025) before the credit.
- Includes attendant care, facility/respite care, prescriptions, devices. Private insurance premiums not eligible.
- [METC (lines 33099/33199) — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/lines-33099-33199-eligible-medical-expenses-you-claim-on-your-tax-return.html)

## Ontario Caregiver Organization (OCO)

- Free helpline **1-833-416-2273** (live chat too). Counselling (SCALE), peer support, e-learning, toolkits, financial-support resources.
- No income test, no cost, confidential.
- [ontariocaregiver.ca](https://ontariocaregiver.ca/)
