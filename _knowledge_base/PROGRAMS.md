## Jurisdiction map

| System | Level | Who administers |
|---|---|---|
| **WSIB** | **Ontario only** | Workplace Safety and Insurance Board |
| **WCB** | Provincial (BC, AB, MB, SK, NS, NB, NL, PE, YT, NT, NU) | Each province's board |
| **VAC** | Federal | Veterans Affairs Canada |
| **ODSP / AISH** | Provincial | Ontario MCCSS / Alberta Human Services |
| **OAS, GIS, CPP, EI, CDB, DTC** | Federal | Service Canada / CRA |
| **Home care, LTC, CHRA** | Ontario / federal split | Ontario Health atHome / CHRC |

⚠️ **Age discrimination split:** the **CHRA** covers only the federal government and
federally regulated organizations. In Ontario, most age-discrimination claims go to the
**HRTO** under the Ontario Human Rights Code — a different body.

### Federal — veterans (VAC)
[Veterans Benefits and Services (VAC)](/knowledge-base/veterans-benefits-vac-canada/)

Disability benefits (Pain and Suffering Compensation / Disability Pension), Treatment
Benefits, Rehabilitation Services, Clothing Allowance, Critical Injury Benefit,
Additional Pain and Suffering Compensation, Guided Support (12 months, Veteran Service
Agent), Attendance Allowance, Exceptional incapacity Allowance, Survivor benefits,
Telemedicine, Financial advice ($500), Disability Pension corrective payment (2003–2010).

**Disability Pension limit:** WWII/Korean War service, or other CAF service with
application **before 1 April 2006**.

## ⚠️ Does NOT exist — do not publish

- **"Participib"** — no federal program by this name was found. Likely confusion with the
  **Canada Workers Benefit** (a tax credit, not a disability benefit).
- **"Ontario Senior Support Program"** — no Government of Ontario program by this name.
  Likely meant Ontario Health atHome, **New Horizons for Seniors**, or **Rapid Access
  Seniors Supports**.
- **"Enhanced GIS"** — not an official program name. What exists: GIS top-up thresholds
  and the permanent 10% OAS increase at 75+.
- **"CPP family caregiver benefit"** — no such federal program. Federal caregiver
  income replacement is **EI only**. Some commercial pages claim otherwise.
