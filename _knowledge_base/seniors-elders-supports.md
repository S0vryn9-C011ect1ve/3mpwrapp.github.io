# Seniors and Elders — Income, Services and Your Rights

**Verified 2026-10-03** against official sources, linked throughout. Amounts marked
**Oct–Dec 2026** are the current quarterly figures. **Not legal advice.**

> ⚠️ **If you were quoted $751.97 or $827.17, those are out of date.** Those were the
> July–September 2026 amounts. The current figures are **$762.50** and **$838.75**.

---

## Old Age Security (OAS) — Canada-wide

- **65 or older.** Canadian citizen or legal resident, resident in Canada **at least
  10 years since age 18** (20 years if living outside Canada).
- **Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:**
  - Age **65–74: $762.50/month**
  - Age **75+: $838.75/month** — permanently 10% higher since July 2022
- **Income cut-off (2025 net world income):** $152,062 (65–74); $157,923 (75+). A
  partial pension applies with 10–40 years of residence.
- Amounts are **taxable**; recovery tax applies above $93,454 (2025 threshold). Indexed
  to CPI quarterly — **up only, never down**.
- **Apply:** usually automatic (Service Canada mails a letter). If nothing arrives a
  month after your 64th birthday, apply in My Service Canada Account or at any
  Service Canada office.
- **Appeal:** reconsideration within **90 days** (form **ISP-3134**) → **Social Security
  Tribunal of Canada**.
- [OAS](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security.html)

## Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) — Canada-wide

**Tax-free top-up** for lower-income OAS recipients. You must **receive OAS** to get it.

**Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:**

| Who | Maximum / month |
|---|---|
| Single, widowed or divorced | **$1,138.90** |
| Spouse of an OAS recipient | **$685.56** |
| Spouse receiving no OAS/Allowance | **$1,138.90** |

**GIS Allowance (ages 60–64):** up to **$1,428.06/month** — a spouse or common-law
partner of an OAS recipient. **Apply 6–11 months before turning 60.**
**Allowance for the Survivor (60–64):** widowed, not remarried, income under $31,152 —
up to **$1,726.18/month**.

> ⚠️ Service Canada states **GIS payments may stop or be reduced if you have not filed
> your 2025 taxes.** File, even if you owe nothing.

**Appeal:** same 90-day reconsideration → SST route as OAS.

[GIS](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/guaranteed-income-supplement.html)

## Canada Pension Plan (CPP)

- **At least 60 years old** plus at least one valid CPP contribution.
- **Maximum new benefits, January 2026:** retirement at 65 **$1,507.65/month**;
  survivor's 65+ $904.59; death benefit $2,500. Benefits in pay indexed **+2.0%**.
- **Appeal:** reconsideration within **90 days** (form **ISP-1238**) → SSTC.
- [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp.html)

> ⚠️ **"Enhanced GIS" is not an official program name.** What exists is the enhanced
> **GIS top-up threshold** (single OAS recipient top-up cut-off **$10,496** for
> Oct–Dec 2026) and the permanent 10% OAS increase at 75+.

## Age discrimination — know which tribunal you use

**Age is a prohibited ground of discrimination** under the **Canadian Human Rights Act**,
with **no upper age limit** — protection does not stop at 65.

> ⚠️ **Jurisdiction matters.** The CHRA covers the **federal government and federally
> regulated organizations** (banks, airlines, rail, telecom, federally regulated media).
> **In Ontario, most employers and services fall under the Ontario Human Rights Code and
> the Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario — a different body.**

- **Federal route:** complaint to the **CHRC** (online/email/fax/mail; no walk-in).
  Deadline generally **one year** from the last act. Process: complaint → CHRC screening
  → Tribunal → respondent response (60 days) → your reply (60 days). CHRC does not
  guarantee processing times.
- **Ontario route:** the **HRTO** under the *Ontario Human Rights Code*.
- [CHRC — about discrimination](https://www.chrc-ccdp.gc.ca/individuals/human-rights/about-discrimination)

## Long-term care and home care — Ontario only

Administered by **Ontario Health atHome** (not "Home Care Ontario" — that name is
outdated and may send people looking for an organization that no longer exists).

### If you need long-term care
Assessment, then **rank up to five homes** in preference order. You have **24 hours** to
accept or reject an offer and up to **5 days to move in**; payment starts on the move-in
date regardless.

**Maximum co-payment fees, effective 1 July 2026** (same for for-profit and
not-for-profit homes):

| Room type | Per day | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| Long-stay basic | **$70.00** | **$2,129.17** |
| Long-stay semi-private | **$84.40** | **$2,567.17** |
| Long-stay private | **$100.01** | **$3,041.97** |
| Short-stay | **$45.31** | — |

The level of care is the same regardless of room type; optional extras (hairdressing,
cable, phone, internet, transportation) can be charged on top.

**Low-income help — Long-Term Care Rate Reduction:** covers **basic accommodation
only** (regular semi-private/private are **not** eligible). No fixed income threshold,
but someone with no dependants would likely qualify below roughly **$27,338** based on
the July 2026 basic rate. Comfort allowance currently **$149/month**. Apply to your home
**within 90 days** of moving in (retroactive up to 90 days), and **re-apply every
year** — to keep the reduced rate from 1 July you must re-apply **between 1 July and
28 September**.

### Home care
For **people of all ages**, not seniors only. If you qualify, **the Ontario government
pays for home care** (community support services may have a co-payment). Call Ontario
Health atHome for a case manager assessment.

**Appeal:** complain to Ontario Health atHome first; if unsatisfied, escalate, then
appeal to the **Ontario Health Services Appeal and Review Board (HSARB)**.

**Community Paramedicine for LTC** is available while on the LTC waitlist — it helps you
stay living at home while waiting.

[Apply for LTC](https://www.ontario.ca/page/apply-long-term-care) ·
[Paying for LTC](https://www.ontario.ca/page/paying-long-term-care) ·
[Home and community care](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-community-care)

## The Age Amount tax credit

**Not a benefit** — it is a federal **non-refundable tax credit** (line 30100).

- **Eligible if you were 65 on 31 December** of the tax year (65 by 31 Dec 2025 →
  claim on your 2025 return filed in 2026) **and** net income under **$105,709**.
- **2025 tax year amount:** full **$9,028** if net income **$45,522 or less**; reduced
  between $45,522 and $105,709.
- ⚠️ The **2026 tax-year** amount was not yet published as of 2026-10-03.
- [CRA — Age Amount](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-30100-amount.html)

## Two programs that do NOT exist

- **There is no "Ontario Senior Support Program."** No Government of Ontario program by
  that name was found. Likely meant: Ontario Health atHome home & community care, the
  federal **New Horizons for Seniors Program**, or **Rapid Access Seniors Supports**
  (some public health units).
- **"Enhanced GIS"** is not an official program name — see above.

## 2026 budget status

As of 2026-10-03 there is **no legislated increase to OAS or GIS dollar amounts** in the
Spring Economic Update 2026. The only OAS-related item is funding for a new OAS
platform and increased processing capacity. The 2026 changes are **CPI indexation
(+1.4% for Oct–Dec 2026, +3.0% year over year)** and a proposed **Canada Groceries and
Essentials benefit** (family of four up to $1,890 this year; single person up to $950),
explicitly **in addition to** GIS and CCB.

## Combining with disability programs

Retirement does not end disability support:

- **OAS/GIS + CPP disability** — both can be claimed.
- **OAS/GIS + the Disability Tax Credit** — the credit applies regardless.
- **Seniors under 65 with a disability** — the
  [non-work disability programs page](/knowledge-base/all-disabilities-non-work-injury-programs/)
  covers CPP disability, EI and the Canada Disability Benefit.
- **Long-term care + GIS** — ask the home about the rate reduction; many people
  qualify and do not apply.
