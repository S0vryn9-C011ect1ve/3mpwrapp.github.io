# Seniors Benefits Canada — Quick Reference

**Verified 2026-10-07** against official sources, linked throughout. Amounts marked **Oct–Dec 2026** are the current quarterly federal figures; **GAINS 2026** is the current benefit year. **Not legal advice.**

> 🔎 These programs stack. You can receive OAS + GIS + GAINS together, and CPP Disability alongside them if you are under 65.

---

## Old Age Security (OAS) — Canada-wide

- **Age:** 65+. Residency: 10 years in Canada since 18 (20 if abroad).
- **Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:** 65–74 **$762.50/mo**; 75+ **$838.75/mo** (permanently +10% since Jul 2022).
- **Taxable.** Recovery tax above 2025 net world income **$93,454**. Indexed quarterly (up only).
- **Apply:** usually automatic after 64th birthday; else My Service Canada Account.
- **Appeal:** reconsideration within **90 days** (form **ISP-3134**) → Social Security Tribunal of Canada.
- [OAS — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security.html)

## Guaranteed Income Supplement (GIS) — Canada-wide

- **Tax-free** top-up; you must receive OAS.
- **Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:** single/widowed/divorced **$1,138.90/mo**; spouse gets full OAS or Allowance **$685.56/mo**; spouse gets no OAS/Allowance **$1,138.90/mo**.
- **File taxes every year** or GIS stops/drops.
- [GIS — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/guaranteed-income-supplement.html)

## The Allowance — Canada-wide

- **Ages 60–64**, low income, spouse receives GIS.
- **Maximum, Oct–Dec 2026:** up to **$1,428.06/mo** (spouse receives GIS and full OAS).
- Apply 6–11 months before turning 60.
- [Allowance — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/guaranteed-income-supplement/allowance.html)

## CPP Disability — for under 65

- **Over 18 and under 65**, severe/prolonged disability preventing substantially all work.
- **Maximum (2026): $1,741.20/mo**; average (Jul 2026) $1,236.32.
- **Appeal:** reconsideration (form **ISP-1145**) → SST General Division within **90 days**.
- Converts to CPP retirement pension at 65.
- [CPP Disability — Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/cpp-disability-benefit.html) · [SST](https://sst-tss.gc.ca/en)

## GAINS — Ontario top-up

- **Age 65+**, Ontario resident, receiving OAS **and** GIS, private income ≤ **$4,416** (single) / ≤ **$8,832** (couple).
- **Maximum 2026 benefit year (Jul 1 2026–Jun 30 2027):** up to **$92/mo** (single) / **$184/mo** (couple). Non-taxable.
- Automatic if OAS+GIS; file taxes yearly.
- **Appeal:** Notice of Objection within **90 days** to Ontario Ministry of Finance (**1-866-668-8297**).
- [GAINS — ontario.ca](https://www.ontario.ca/page/guaranteed-annual-income-system-payments-seniors)

## Long-term care, capacity & CCB

- **Consent and Capacity Board (CCB)** — capacity, consent to treatment, committal, findings of incapacity to make LTC decisions: **[ccboard.on.ca](https://www.ccboard.on.ca/)** (note: *not* under tribunalsontario.ca — that slug is dead).
- **LTC home placement/admission appeals** — Health Services Appeal and Review Board (HSARB), administered within **[Tribunals Ontario](https://tribunalsontario.ca/en)**; confirm the assigned tribunal.
- Free elder-law help: **Advocacy Centre for the Elderly** [acelaw.ca](https://www.acelaw.ca/).
