# WSIB Strike — Appeals Paused: What Injured Workers Need to Know

## 📋 What This Guide Covers

Since **May 22, 2025**, over **4,000 WSIB employees** represented by the **Ontario Public Service Employees Union (OPSEU)** have been on strike. The WSIB has **suspended all appeals processes and decision-making** for the duration of the labour disruption.

In September 2026, the situation is compounded by confirmed **layoff reports**: WSIB offices in Thunder Bay, St. Catharines, and Sudbury are closing, with 400+ jobs cut and some IT/claims functions relocated to Texas and Boston.

---

## ⚠️ Key Impacts for Injured Workers

### Appeals & Hearings — On Hold
- **WSIAT ( Workplace Safety and Insurance Appeals Tribunal)** hearings are **paused** pending claim file updates from WSIB.
- **WSIB internal appeals** (Reconsiderations) are **suspended**.
- **No new hearing dates** have been set — the WSIB will contact you to reschedule when the labour disruption ends.

### Deadline Extensions — Unclear
- **Objection time limits** are temporarily extended, but **details are unclear** and vary by case type.
- **Do not assume automatic extensions** — document all deadlines in your Evidence Locker.
- If you have a filing deadline during this period, **mark it immediately** and contact your representative.

### Claim Processing — Minimal
- The WSIB is maintaining **critical services only** (urgent medical reports, immediate benefit payments).
- **Routine claim adjudication** (new claims, rate calculations, form updates) is **delayed**.
- **Annual reviews** and **redeterminations** are **paused**.

### Service Access — Reduced
- WSIB offices in affected regions are **closed or operating with skeleton staff**.
- Phone lines may experience **longer wait times**.
- **Online services** (My Benefits, eForms) remain available but with **slower processing**.

---

## ✅ What You CAN Do

### 1. Document Everything
- Use **Evidence Locker** to scan and timestamp all correspondence, forms, and medical reports.
- Create a dedicated tag: `WSIB Strike / Appeal Paused / Sept 2026`.
- Save any **deadline notices** you receive — these are your proof of timely action.

### 2. Track Your Deadlines
- The WSIB strike **extends objection periods**, but you must still file within the extended windows.
- Use the **Deadline Calculator** in 3mpwrApp to track your personal deadlines.
- If a deadline passes during the strike, note the reason — the labour disruption may excuse a late filing if you can prove you tried.

### 3. Contact Your Union (If Applicable)
- If you are a **public sector worker** covered by OPSEU, check with your local for strike support updates.
- If you are not unionized, reach out to your **Injured Worker Group (IIG)** or the **Ontario Network of Injured Workers Groups (ONIWG)**.

### 4. Keep Your Lawyer/Representative Informed
- Forward this resource to anyone representing you.
- If you have a **WSIAT hearing** already scheduled, ask your representative to request a **continuance** or written decision in your absence.
