# Which System Applies to Me?

**Start here if you are not sure where to apply.** Most people are told about *one*
system and never learn another one exists. This page sorts out which door is yours.

> **Not legal advice.** This routes you to the right starting point. Program rules
> change — confirm with the administering body before acting.

---

## The one question that decides everything

**What caused your disability or injury?**

| If the cause was... | The system is... | You start at... |
|---|---|---|
| **Your job** | **WSIB** (Ontario) or the **WCB** in your province | The board for your province |
| **Military service** | **VAC** (Veterans Affairs Canada) | [Veterans Affairs Canada](https://veterans.gc.ca/en/contact-us) |
| **Neither — illness, condition, or circumstance** | **Disability income support** (provincial or federal) | [ODSP](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-disability-support-program) · [AISH](https://www.alberta.ca/adult-integrated-services-income-support) · [CPP disability](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/cppenei/income-disability-benefits.html) · [EI sickness](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-sickness-benefits.html) |
| **Age (65+)** | **Senior benefits** | [Old Age Security](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/cppenei/oas.html) |
| **Someone else's situation** | **Family/caregiver supports** | [Family & caregivers](https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/families-and-caregivers) and provincial caregiver programs |

## The mistake that costs people the most

**Work injury and service injury are separate systems. A decision from one says nothing about the other.**

If you are a veteran who was injured on the job:

- You may have a **WSIB claim** (workplace)
- You may also have a **VAC claim** (service-related)
- They are **not substitutes.** A WSIB denial does not decide your VAC claim.

If you were injured at work but **have no WSIB coverage** (self-employed, informal
work, or outside Canada), that is **not** the end of it. You may still qualify for
VAC, provincial disability support, CPP disability, or the Disability Tax Credit.

## Start with the one that fits

### 🦯 I was injured at work
→ **WSIB** (Ontario) or your provincial **WCB**.
Your employer files the report. You then file a claim with the board.
- **Ontario:** [WSIB](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90w1b) · Tribunal: [WSIAT](https://www.wsiat.ca/)
- Find your province's board: workers' compensation boards are provincial — search
  "[your province] workers compensation board."
- **Already have a claim?** The injury guides and appeals material are in the
  [Knowledge Base home page](/knowledge-base/).

### 🎖️ I served in the military
→ **VAC**. Eligibility needs a **diagnosed condition** *and* a connection to your service.
- Full breakdown: **[Veterans Benefits and Services (VAC)](/knowledge-base/veterans-benefits-vac-canada/)**
- Free help with an application: The Royal Canadian Legion and The War Amps assist at no charge.

### 🧠 I have a disability that is not work- or service-related
→ Income support varies by province. **You are not out of options.**
- **Ontario:** [Ontario Disability Support Program (ODSP)](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-disability-support-program)
- **Alberta:** [AISH](https://www.alberta.ca/adult-integrated-services-income-support)
- **All provinces/CPP:** [CPP disability benefits](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/cppenei/income-disability-benefits.html)
- **Short-term:** [EI sickness benefits](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-sickness-benefits.html)
- **Tax relief:** [Disability Tax Credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/segments/tax-credits-deductions-persons-disabilities/disability-tax-credit.html) (CRA)

### 👴 I am 65+ or retiring
→ [Old Age Security](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/cppenei/oas.html), including the
**Guaranteed Income Supplement** for lower incomes. Age discrimination in employment
and services is prohibited under the [Canadian Human Rights Act](https://www.chrc-ccdp.gc.ca/).

### 👥 I am caring for someone with a disability
→ Family and caregiver programs exist and are frequently overlooked. See the
family/caregiver section of the knowledge base.

## Common combinations

You do **not** have to pick one. These overlap:

- **Work injury + other health issues** — WSIB for the work injury; provincial
  disability support or CPP for conditions that aren't work-related.
- **Service injury + work injury** — a VAC claim **and** a WSIB claim, both valid.
- **Disability + low income** — income support (ODSP/AISH) **and** the Disability
  Tax Credit **and** provincial or federal tax credits.
- **Senior + disability** — OAS/GIS **and** CPP disability **and** the Disability
  Tax Credit.

## If you are denied

Every one of these systems has an appeal route, and **the route differs by system**:

| System | Appeal route |
|---|---|
| **WSIB** (Ontario) | Internal review, then **WSIAT** |
| **WCB** (other provinces) | The provincial board's review and appeal process |
| **VAC** | **Departmental review** |
| **CPP disability** | **Dispute Resolution Appeals Board (DRAB)** |
| **ODSP / AISH** | The provincial internal review process |

**A denial is not always the end.** Ask for the reasons in writing, check whether
the decision followed the rules, and consider a representative.
