# Family and Caregiver Supports

**For the people doing the caring** — spouses, parents, adult children, and anyone
else supporting someone with a disability or serious illness.

> **Verified 2026-10-03** against official sources, linked throughout.
> **Not legal advice.** Caregiving is unpaid work that people rarely claim support for.

---

## EI Caregiving Benefits — Canada-wide

Service Canada pays three separate benefits. All are **55% of earnings, maximum
$729/week (2026)**.

| Benefit | Maximum weeks | For |
|---|---|---|
| **EI Family Support Benefit** | up to **35 weeks** | Children |
| **EI Adult Support** | up to **15 weeks** | A spouse or adult dependent |
| **Compassionate Care Benefit** | up to **26 weeks** | A family member who is seriously ill or dying |

**Two access facts people often miss:**
- You **do not need to be related** to the person you are caring for, and you **do not
  need to live with them.**
- For the **Compassionate Care Benefit**, a chronic condition alone does **not**
  qualify — it requires a **new acute life-threatening change**, or an existing
  condition that has sharply worsened.

**Apply** through My Service Canada Account or a Service Canada office. You will need a
medical certificate for the person you are caring for.

**Appeal:** reconsideration within **30 days** (form **INS5210**), no fee. ⚠️ Since
**1 April 2026**, EI appeals go to the **EI Benefits Appeal Tribunal (BOA)**, not the
Social Security Tribunal.

[EI compassionate care](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-compassionate-care.html)

## The Canada Caregiver Credit — Canada-wide

A federal **tax credit**, not an income replacement.

- **$2,687** base.
- Up to **$8,601** for a spouse or common-law partner, or an adult dependant.
- **$2,687 per child.**
- **"Dependent"** means someone who **regularly and consistently relies on you** for
  food, shelter or clothing.

Claim it on your tax return.

## VAC Caregiver Recognition Benefit — veterans' families

- **$1,264.25/month**, tax-free, to the unpaid family caregiver of a qualifying
  veteran.
- ⚠️ **Eligibility trap:** this is for veterans receiving a **Disability Award or Pain
  and Suffering Compensation**. Veterans who receive the **Disability Pension** get the
  **Attendance Allowance** instead. Check which benefit the veteran actually receives.

## Respite and breaks in Ontario

Respite is care for the person you care for, so that you can rest. It is far
under-used.

- **In your home:** fully covered if approved.
- **Long-term care short stay:** **maximum 60 days per stay, 90 days per calendar
  year.** ⚠️ **Only accommodation costs are paid** — you may have to cover other costs.
- **Adult day programs:** available through an **Ontario Health atHome referral**,
  usually with a daily fee.

**Start with Ontario Health atHome** — an assessment opens the respite options.

## Powers of attorney in Ontario — two traps

1. ⚠️ **Land registry rejects powers of attorney containing personal information.**
   Do **not** attach ID copies, bank account numbers, passports or birth certificates.
   A POA containing personal information can be refused at land registration.
2. A property attorney can act **immediately** unless you write an **incapacity
   trigger** into the document. Decide which you want — it changes when the document
   takes effect.

Ontario also recognises **continuing power of attorney** and **springing power of
attorney**. A court-appointed guardian/trustee/committee, a protection mandate, or a
continuing/springing POA can act as a legal representative on benefit applications —
but an **authorized person** cannot apply, change banking, or request a formal
reconsideration.

## Caregiving combined with your own disability

If you are both a caregiver and a person with a disability, these are separate claims:

- **Your own** disability support: see
  [non-work disability programs](/knowledge-base/all-disabilities-non-work-injury-programs/)
  — the Canada Disability Benefit does **not** reduce Ontario social assistance.
- **Caregiving support:** EI Family Support / Compassionate Care, or the VAC Caregiver
  Recognition Benefit.
- **Your own retirement income:** see
  [seniors and elders supports](/knowledge-base/seniors-elders-supports/).

## One thing that does NOT exist

⚠️ **There is no "CPP family caregiver benefit."** No such federal program exists —
canada.ca and Service Canada have no such benefit. Federal caregiver **income
replacement** is **EI only**. Some commercial pages claim a CPP caregiver benefit
exists; that is misinformation.

If you saw it elsewhere, this is worth knowing: people lose real money planning around
a benefit that was never created.

## Start here

1. **Are you caring for a child, an adult, or someone seriously ill?** → EI Family
   Support (35 weeks), Adult Support (15 weeks), or Compassionate Care (26 weeks).
2. **Do you need a break?** → Ask Ontario Health atHome about respite.
3. **Do you have legal authority for someone?** → Ontario POA, and check the land
   registry trap above.
4. **Are you a veteran's family?** → Check whether they receive Disability Award/Pain
   and Suffering (you may get Caregiver Recognition) or Disability Pension (Attendance
   Allowance instead).
