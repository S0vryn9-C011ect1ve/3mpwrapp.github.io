# Federal Disability Programs — Quick Reference

A fast lookup of Canada's main federal disability programs. All figures are sourced from
official Canada.ca pages and are current as of **October 7, 2026**. Amounts and thresholds
are indexed and change yearly — confirm on the linked source before relying on a number.

## At a glance

| Program | Type | 2025–2026 key figure | Core eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CPP Disability (CPP-D)](#cpp-disability-cpp-d) | Monthly taxable pension | Max **$1,741.20/mo** (2026); avg $1,236.32/mo (Jul 2026) | Under 65; severe & prolonged disability; enough CPP contributions |
| [Disability Tax Credit (DTC)](#disability-tax-credit-dtc) | Non-refundable tax credit | **$10,138** (2025, line 31600); child supplement $5,914 | Severe & prolonged impairment, practitioner-certified (T2201) |
| [Canada Disability Benefit (CDB)](#canada-disability-benefit-cdb) | Monthly income-tested benefit | Max **$200/mo** (Jul2025–Jun2026); **$204.20/mo** (Jul2026–Jun2027) | Ages 18–64; DTC approved; tax return filed |
| [RDSP](#registered-disability-savings-plan-rdsp) | Long-term savings + grants/bonds | Grant ≤ **$3,500/yr** (life $70k); Bond ≤ **$1,000/yr** (life $20k) | DTC approved; under 60 to open |
| [Canadian Human Rights Tribunal (CHRT)](#canadian-human-rights-tribunal-chrt) | Federal tribunal | Adjudicates under *Canadian Human Rights Act* | Federally regulated areas; complaint filed with CHRC first |

> **The DTC is the hinge:** DTC approval unlocks the RDSP and the CDB. Apply for it first.

## CPP Disability (CPP-D)

- **Source:** [Canada.ca — CPP disability benefits](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/cpp-disability-benefit.html)
- **Eligibility:** over 18 and under 65; a **severe and prolonged** disability that prevents
  substantially working and is long-term; sufficient CPP contributions.
- **Amounts:** 2026 maximum **$1,741.20/month**; July 2026 average **$1,236.32/month**.
- **Apply:** [My Service Canada Account](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/my-account.html)
  (application + medical report). Reconsideration → [Social Security Tribunal](https://www.sst-tss.ca/).

## Disability Tax Credit (DTC)

- **Source:** [Canada.ca — Disability tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/segments/tax-credits-deductions-persons-disabilities/disability-tax-credit.html)
- **What it is:** non-refundable federal tax credit; reduces tax owed; **gateway** to RDSP and CDB.
- **2025 amounts:** disability amount **$10,138** (line 31600); supplement for child under 18
  **$5,914**. Indexed annually.
- **Apply:** Form T2201, certified by a practitioner; digital or mail (since July 14, 2025
  you can no longer upload via CRA "submit documents").

## Canada Disability Benefit (CDB)

- **Source:** [Canada.ca — Canada Disability Benefit](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/disability/canada-disability-benefit.html) · [Amount](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/disability/canada-disability-benefit/amount.html)
- **Launched:** first eligibility **June 2025**; payments began **July 2025**.
- **Eligibility:** ages **18–64**; DTC approved; federal tax return filed (you + spouse if any).
- **Maximum monthly:** **$200.00** (Jul 2025–Jun 2026, from 2024 return); **$204.20**
  (Jul 2026–Jun 2027, from 2025 return). Income-tested; working-income exemption
  ($10,000 single / $14,000 couple for 2025–26). Back payments up to 24 months.
- **Supplemental:** one-time **$150** payment (Fall 2026) toward DTC costs; no separate application.

## Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP)

- **Source:** [Canada.ca — RDSP](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/disability/savings.html) · [Grants & bonds](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/disability/savings/grants-bonds.html)
- **Requires:** DTC approval.
- **Grant:** max **$3,500/yr**, life **$70,000**; matching $3:$1 (first $500) then $2:$1
  (next $1,000) — contribute $1,500/yr for the max. Paid to Dec 31 of year beneficiary turns 49.
- **Bond:** max **$1,000/yr**, life **$20,000**; no contribution needed; low/modest income.
- **Lifetime contribution limit:** **$200,000**.

## Canadian Human Rights Tribunal (CHRT)

- **Source:** [chrt-tcdp.gc.ca](https://www.chrt-tcdp.gc.ca/) · [Process guide](https://www.chrt-tcdp.gc.ca/en/human-rights/process) · [Decisions](https://decisions.chrt-tcdp.gc.ca/chrt-tcdp/en/nav.do)
- **Role:** adjudicates under the *Canadian Human Rights Act* in federally regulated areas.
- **Path:** file complaint with the **Canadian Human Rights Commission (CHRC)** first →
  investigation/conciliation → possible referral to CHRT for hearing.
- **2026 stats:** ~280 active files; 117 decisions/rulings issued.
