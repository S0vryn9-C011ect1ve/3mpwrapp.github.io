# 🔬 Research

Every figure in the 3mpwrapp guides traces back to analysed decision data. This is the
hub for that work — check it rather than take the guides on trust.

**3mpwrapp serves:** injured workers & WSIB/WSIAT claimants, veterans (VAC/VRAB),
seniors, caregivers, persons with disabilities, public allies, and the human-rights /
UNCRPD / legal communities. The resources below are grouped by community so you can find
the guides, knowledge bases, and templates that apply to you.

## 🔄 CanLII decision-data scraping — resuming soon

We are **resuming CanLII decision-data collection** to keep the visualizations and
outcome models current. Status:

- ✅ **British Columbia workers' compensation (WCAT) 2020–2026 — already collected**: 7,386 decisions,
  scraped via the free CanLII API (no paid scraping).
- 🔜 **Next:** complete the rest of BC (Human Rights Tribunal, Information & Privacy), then expand
  across Canada — **AB, QC, MB, SK, Atlantic, and the Territories** — plus the **ON Landlord &
  Tenant Board** (poverty / housing).
- 🔒 All collection uses the **free CanLII API** with a 15-second rate limit. We are **not** consuming
  paid Firecrawl credits (conserved at 95%). Runs are scheduled as a daily cron, one jurisdiction per day
  to stay within CanLII quota.

See [Data Sources & Methodology](/docs/DATA_SOURCES_METHODOLOGY/) for how scraped data becomes the
figures you see in the guides.

## ⚠️ A note on the numbers

Tribunal outcome data is complex. Win-rate figures on this site are **exploratory** and
derived from keyword-classified decision subsets — they are **not** official adjudicative
rates. Where a page states a precise percentage, it is labelled as inferred, not verified.
The single source of truth for every stat is
[Data Sources & Methodology](/docs/DATA_SOURCES_METHODOLOGY/).

**Not legal advice.** Research here helps you understand patterns and prepare evidence.
It is not a substitute for a qualified legal or paralegal representative.
